Types: a savings plan execution ("Sparplan", "PAC") is a buy. A distribution or coupon is a
dividend. Withholding tax, capital gains tax, solidarity surcharge, church tax and stamp duty
("Kapitalertragsteuer", "Soli", "Kirchensteuer", "Bollo", "Ritenuta") are tax. A securities
transfer from or to another depot ("Depotübertrag", "Einlieferung", "Auslieferung",
"trasferimento titoli") is transferIn or transferOut. Use "other" for anything else, and keep
the printed word in typeText.

One line per transaction: when a buy, its fee and its tax are printed as one entry, write one
buy with fees and tax filled in. When they're printed as separate entries, write them
separately.
