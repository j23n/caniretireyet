Last Monday answers one question: when can you stop working? It turns your accounts and your plans into a date, and moves that date a little every month.

Find your date See how it works

For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Five minutes a month, and the date moves.

Every month you record what you have. The plan runs again, and the check-in ends with your date: sooner, later, or the same.

Thousands of futures, one date.

Markets don't move in a straight line, so the app runs your plan through 2,000 simulated ones. Your last Monday is the earliest that still works in 9 of 10.

Decades shows your records up to now, then the range of futures to 95, with the chapters of your plan underneath.

No account, no server

Nothing to sign up for. Your iPhone, iPad and Mac read the same folder, and iCloud keeps it in sync.
