Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: Last Monday doesn't collect your personal data. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your accounts, values and plans are files in your own iCloud Drive, and we can't see them.

This policy covers the Last Monday app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, its widgets, and this website. "We" means the developer of Last Monday, [developer name], who you can reach at [contact email].

Your financial data

Everything you enter or import (accounts, balances, trades, prices, plans and settings) is stored as plain JSON files in a folder called Last Monday in your iCloud Drive, or on your device if you choose not to use iCloud. Apple's iCloud keeps the folder in sync between your devices, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The files never pass through a server of ours, and we have no way to read them.

You can open, copy, export or delete the files at any time. Deleting the Last Monday folder in iCloud Drive and removing the app deletes all of it.

Prices, exchange rates and inflation

To value your holdings, the app asks these services for prices directly from your device:

ServiceWhat it's asked for
Yahoo FinancePrices of shares, funds and crypto, and the search for a ticker symbol
FrankfurterExchange rates, from the European Central Bank's reference rates
Gold APIPrices of gold and other precious metals
Eurostat, US Bureau of Labor Statistics, UK Office for National StatisticsInflation indexes

A request names only what it needs: a ticker symbol, a currency pair or an index, and the dates. It never contains your amounts, quantities, account names or plans. Like any request on the internet, it reaches the service from your IP address, and that service's own privacy policy applies. You can turn automatic price updates off in the app's Settings and type prices yourself.

Purchases

Last Monday Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app learns only whether you have Plus, through Apple's StoreKit. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. App Store Connect gives us sales reports that don't identify you.

Notifications and widgets

The monthly check-in reminder and the other notes are scheduled on your device, and show ages and years, never amounts. The widgets read a small summary that the app keeps on your device. Neither involves a server.

Crash reports and beta feedback

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple sends us crash reports and diagnostics that don't identify you. If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple gives us what you send: your comment, a screenshot if you add one, and basic device information. We use it only to fix and improve the app, and keep it in a private issue tracker. A screenshot shows what's on screen, so hide amounts with the eye button before you take one.

What you share yourself

Export Calculations and the CSV export write files that you choose where to save or send. Export Calculations can leave out names and exact dates, and round amounts, if you turn on Anonymize. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log your IP address to keep the service running and secure, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts.

Children

Last Monday isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data, children's included.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you beyond any email or feedback you've sent us, there's usually nothing for us to hand over or delete. If you've written to us and want a copy of that correspondence, or want it deleted, write to [contact email]. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. If a change means the app would collect data it doesn't today, the app will ask you first.
