Join the beta

Last Monday is in beta on TestFlight for iPhone, iPad and Mac while it gets ready for the App Store. Join the beta on TestFlight. It needs iOS 26 or macOS 26.

To send feedback from the beta, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Hide amounts with the eye button first if you'd rather they stayed private.

Where are my files?

In iCloud Drive, in a folder called Last Monday. On a Mac, open it in the Finder's sidebar under iCloud Drive. On iPhone and iPad, open the Files app and go to iCloud Drive. Each file is plain JSON, so you can read it, copy it or keep it in your own backups.

How do I move to a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, and install Last Monday. It finds the Last Monday folder and opens your library. Nothing needs to be exported.

Prices aren't updating

Prices update when a check-in opens and after each import, if automatic updates are on in Settings. Open the price list from the check-in to see where each price came from, or why it couldn't be fetched. For an instrument without a price source, add its ticker symbol on the Instruments screen, or type the price yourself.

I bought Plus, but the app doesn't show it

Open Settings in the app, tap Get Plus, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

In your Apple Account's subscription settings: on iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Your plans and files stay as they are when Plus ends.

Why does my date say 9 of 10 futures?

The app runs your plan through 2,000 simulated markets. Your last Monday is the earliest date at which your money lasts to the plan's end in at least 9 of 10 of them. You can change the assumptions behind it, such as inflation and market returns, in the plan.

Is this financial advice?

No. The answers are estimates from a simplified model. See the Terms of Use.
